Blast Radius Framework™

Know your blast radius.

A structured AWS security assessment that models every propagation path, scores your containment strength, and gives you a sequenced plan to compress exposure — before an incident does it for you.

View Sample Report Schedule an Assessment

Security tools find vulnerabilities. BRF maps their blast radius.

Your CSPM flags misconfigurations. Your pentest finds entry points. What neither answers: how far can an attacker move before you stop them? BRF models the full propagation graph — from initial compromise to org-admin — so you know exactly what's at risk, how fast, and what actually stops the spread.

Four phases. One complete picture.

  1. Structural Surface Mapping

    Map every trust relationship, entry class, and privilege boundary in your account. Cross-account trust, environment separation, SAML federation, and CI/CD access chains — fully enumerated and risk-scored.

  2. Propagation Modeling

    Score and rank every viable blast path from each entry class. Each path shows the full propagation chain, a BPSS severity score, hops-to-admin metric, and linked evidence.

  3. Containment Strength Evaluation

    Quantify your ability to stop propagation across five domains. The Containment Strength Index (CSI) gives you a single score — and a breakdown that shows exactly where to invest.

  4. Risk Compression Plan

    Interventions ranked by impact-to-effort ratio, not arbitrary priority. A sequenced 60-day remediation plan, tied to specific blast paths, that reduces the most risk for the least work.

One engagement. Six deliverables.

  • Blast Path Register — 20+ propagation paths, each scored by severity band and entry class.
  • Containment Strength Index — A 0–100 score across Identity, Network, Data, Compute, and Control domains.
  • Ranked Intervention Matrix — Every control ranked by impact-to-effort ratio.
  • 60-Day Remediation Sequence — A week-by-week plan tied to specific blast paths.
  • Topology & Trust Map — Graph of every node, trust relationship, and privilege boundary.
  • Evidence Package — Every finding backed by AWS API data, hash-verified and timestamped.
